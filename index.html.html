There are things I want you to know, even if I don't always find the perfect words to say them.

Dear Asher, I know we haven't even known each other for a whole month yet. Maybe to some people, that sounds like such a short time. I know we're still learning about each other's little habits, thoughts, fears, and everything in between. And I don't expect us to have everything figured out already. I just feel that what we have is worth taking a step forward for. I want us to keep discovering each other, this time with something more meaningful between us. I catch myself smiling for no reason, thinking about you, or feeling a little silly because I just miss you. I care about you more than I can properly explain, and I appreciate you in ways I hope you never have to question. I love the way you treat me. I love your sweetness, your silliness, your pretty little ways, and all the things that make you who you are. I adore how lovely you are, even in the moments when you probably don't see it yourself. Every little moment with you has become a favorite of mine, without exception. I want to be someone who makes room for every version of you. Not only the happy and confident one, but also the uncertain, tired, and scared one. I wish I could hold your fears gently and remind you that you don't have to carry everything alone. I understand that you have your own responsibilities, your own days, and a life beyond us. I would never want my affection to feel like another obligation. I don't need all of your time. I just hope we keep choosing to communicate, making an effort, and finding our way back to each other even on the busiest days. I don't want to rush the process of knowing you. I want to grow through it with you. I want more ordinary days, silly conversations, little updates, and memories that we'll look back on someday. And if you're willing, I'd love for this to become something we both get to call ours. With all the love I have,
Yours, Malik
